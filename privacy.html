GymQuest Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026

GymQuest stores your onboarding choices, lesson progress, XP, streak, hearts, gems, goals, reminders, and subscription entitlement status on your device. Payments are handled by Apple through StoreKit. GymQuest does not currently operate a custom account backend, collect medical records, or read/write Apple Health data.

Data Stored On Device

GymQuest stores progress and preferences locally using Apple-provided storage. This includes your display name, training goal, training level, lesson progress, daily goal, streak, XP, workout history, reminder preference, and premium entitlement state.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. GymQuest does not receive or store your payment card number.

Notifications

If enabled, workout reminders are scheduled locally on your device and can be disabled in the app or iOS Settings. GymQuest shows an in-app confirmation when reminders are scheduled or removed.

Health and Sensitive Data

GymQuest provides general fitness education. The app does not currently collect medical records, sensitive health data, Apple Health data, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Sharing

GymQuest only shares progress text when you choose to use the native share sheet.

Children

GymQuest is not intended for children under 13.

Contact

Email support@gymlingo.app for privacy questions.
