Need help? Email support@gymlingo.app.

Subscription Not Unlocking

Open Profile and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the subscription.

Cancel Subscription

Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, then select GymLingo.

Workout Reminders

Open Profile and tap Enable Workout Reminder. GymLingo shows a confirmation alert when reminders are scheduled or removed. If reminders do not appear, check iOS Settings, Notifications, GymLingo, and confirm notifications are allowed.

Reset Progress

Open Profile and tap Reset Progress.

Safety

GymLingo is general fitness education, not medical advice.
